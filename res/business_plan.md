4	MISSION STATEMENT / OBJECTIVES/ CORE VALUES:	
4.1	Mission Statement
It is the aim of Aion Health Care & Support Services Limited to provide compassionate, high-quality, and individualised home care and supported living services that empower clients to live independently, safely, and with dignity in their homes. Our vision is to be the leading provider of personal care services in home and supported living environments, recognised for excellence, innovation and person-centred care. This will be achieved by promoting a standard of excellence that embraces fundamental principles of Good Care Practice, which are witnessed and evaluated through the practice, conduct, and control of quality care in the domestic environment.

To meet these service user needs, the Care Service is designed to achieve the following objectives:

•	To deliver a service of the highest quality that will improve and sustain the service user’s overall quality of life. In this respect, the Health and Social Care Service is designed to meet the Certification requirements of the ISO 9000 Quality Standard (latest edition) - as it applies to the Health and Social Care sector - but in a people-oriented fashion.

•	To ensure that the service is delivered flexibly, attentively, and in a non-discriminatory fashion while making choices and taking risks.

•	To ensure that each service user’s needs and values are respected in matters of religion, culture, race or ethnic origin, sexuality and sexual orientation, political affiliation, marital status, parenthood, and disabilities or impairments.

•	To ensure that the Care Service is delivered under agreed Purchasing Contracts / Care Agreements.

•	To manage and implement a formal program of staff planning, selection, recruitment, training, and personal development to enable service user care needs to be met.

•	To match the nominated Care Worker as closely as possible with the service user and respect the need to change the Care Worker in the event of subsequent non-compatibility.

•	To manage the Care Service efficiently and effectively to make the best use of resources and to maximise value for money for the Purchaser of the Service.

•	To undertake a Risk Assessment of Environmental Health & Safety hazards within the home of each new service user, and to ensure that areas of concern are duly reported to the Purchaser of the Service. Such Risk Assessments will consider the right of the service user to take risks, as per the relevant guidelines. Clause 2. Above.

•	To ensure that all service users receive written information on the Organisation’s Procedure for Handling Complaints, Comments, and Compliments, and how to use it.
