Our Story

Aion Health Care & Support Services Limited was founded on the principle that everyone deserves compassionate, high-quality care that allows them to live with dignity and independence in their own homes.

Based in Chelmsford, Essex, our company serves the East of England with a focus on providing comprehensive home care and supported living services. We understand that each person's needs are unique, and we're committed to delivering personalized care that makes a real difference.

Our founding directors, Oluwatosin Alaba Oyewunmi & Dolapo Adenike Ngadaonye, bring over 21 years of combined experience in health and social care. This deep understanding of the sector enables us to provide exceptional care and support to our clients and their families.

Our Mission

It is the aim of Aion Health Care & Support Services Limited to provide compassionate, high-quality, and individualised home care and supported living services that empower clients to live independently, safely, and with dignity in their homes.

Our vision is to be the leading provider of personal care services in home and supported living environments, recognised for excellence, innovation and person-centred care.

Our Objectives

  • Deliver the highest quality care that improves and sustains each service user's overall quality of life
  • Ensure flexible, attentive, non-discriminatory service delivery
  • Respect each service user's needs, values, and personal choices
  • Provide services under agreed care agreements
  • Maintain efficient, effective management for best value
