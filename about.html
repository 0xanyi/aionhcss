Our Story

Founded on the principle that everyone deserves compassionate, high-quality care

Our Foundation

Aion Health Care & Support Services Limited was founded on the principle that everyone deserves compassionate, high-quality care that allows them to live with dignity and independence in their own homes.

Our Location

Based in Chelmsford, Essex, our company serves the East of England with a focus on providing comprehensive home care and supported living services. We understand that each person's needs are unique.

Our Mission & Vision

Dedicated to providing exceptional, person-centred care that transforms lives

Our Mission

To provide compassionate, high-quality, and individualised home care and supported living services that empower clients to live independently, safely, and with dignity in their homes.

Our Vision

To be the leading provider of personal care services in home and supported living environments, recognised for excellence, innovation and person-centred care across the East of England.

Our Key Objectives

Quality Care

Flexible Service

Our Core Values

These values guide everything we do and shape how we interact with our clients, their families, and our community

Compassion

Every interaction is guided by genuine care, empathy, and understanding. We treat each person as an individual with their own unique needs and preferences.

Respect

We honor each person's dignity, independence, and personal choices. Every decision considers their wishes, cultural background, and individual values.

Integrity

Honest, transparent communication forms the foundation of all our relationships. We do what we say we'll do and maintain the highest ethical standards.

Professionalism

Our team consists of highly trained, DBS-cleared professionals committed to continuous learning and delivering excellence in all aspects of care.

Inclusivity

We celebrate diversity and provide culturally sensitive care that respects differences in background, beliefs, lifestyle, and personal preferences.

Dignity

Preserving and promoting each person's dignity is at the heart of everything we do. We support independence while providing just the right amount of assistance.
