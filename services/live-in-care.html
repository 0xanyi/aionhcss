🏠 Live-in Care Services

24/7 Care in the Comfort of Home

Comprehensive round-the-clock care and companionship in your own home, providing peace of mind for you and your family with dedicated, professional support.

What is Live-in Care?

Round-the-clock professional support allowing you to maintain independence in the comfort of your own home

Stay in Your Own Home

Live-in care provides round-the-clock professional support in your own home, allowing you to maintain your independence, familiar routines, and the comfort of living in a place you love.

Dedicated Support

A dedicated care worker lives with you, offering continuous support while respecting your privacy and personal space. This allows couples to stay together, pets to remain in their familiar environment, and you to maintain control over your daily life.

Our Live-in Care Services

Comprehensive 24/7 support tailored to your needs

24-Hour Support & Monitoring

Emergency response and safety monitoring, medication management, assistance with daily activities, regular health checks, and coordination with healthcare professionals around the clock.

Personal Care & Hygiene

Discreet, dignified assistance with bathing, dressing, grooming, toileting and continence care, mobility and transfer support, and exercise assistance.

Domestic Support

Help maintaining your home environment including meal planning and preparation, light housekeeping, laundry and ironing, shopping and errands, and garden maintenance.

Companionship & Social Support

Meaningful social interaction, conversation and emotional support, accompanied outings and appointments, activity engagement and hobbies, and family liaison.

Who Benefits from Live-in Care?

Live-in care is ideal for those needing continuous support in their own home

Complex Health Needs

Individuals with complex health needs requiring regular monitoring and professional care support throughout the day and night.

Safety Concerns

Those at risk of falls or other safety concerns who benefit from having someone nearby at all times for immediate assistance.

Dementia Care

People living with dementia who benefit from consistent familiar faces and continuous support in their own environment.

Couples Support

Couples where one partner needs significant support, allowing them to stay together in their own home rather than being separated.

Live-in Care vs Care Home

Understanding the advantages of remaining in your own home with live-in care support

Stay in Your Own Home

Remain in familiar surroundings with your possessions, memories, and community connections intact rather than moving to unfamiliar settings.

One-to-One Care

Receive dedicated attention from your care worker rather than sharing attention with multiple residents in a care home setting.

Maintain Your Routine

Keep your own meal times, bedtime, and daily routine that works best for you without adhering to institutional schedules.

Keep Your Pets

Continue caring for beloved pets in their familiar environment with your support, providing comfort and companionship.
