Live-in Care Services

Comprehensive 24-hour care and companionship in the comfort of your own home, providing peace of mind for you and your family with dedicated, professional support.

What is Live-in Care?

Live-in care provides round-the-clock professional support in your own home, allowing you to maintain your independence, familiar routines, and the comfort of living in a place you love. A dedicated care worker lives with you, offering continuous support while respecting your privacy and personal space.

This type of care is ideal for individuals who need regular assistance but want to avoid moving to a care home. Live-in care allows couples to stay together, pets to remain in their familiar environment, and for you to maintain control over your daily life and decisions.

Who Benefits from Live-in Care?

Live-in care is particularly beneficial for:

  • Individuals with complex health needs requiring regular monitoring
  • Those at risk of falls or other safety concerns
  • People living with dementia who benefit from consistent familiar faces
  • Couples where one partner needs significant support
  • Individuals who feel isolated or lonely
  • Those who prefer the comfort and familiarity of their own home
  • People who want to avoid moving to a care home

Live-in Care vs Care Home

Understanding the advantages of remaining in your own home with live-in care support.

Stay in Your Own Home

Remain in familiar surroundings with your possessions, memories, and community connections intact.

One-to-One Care

Receive dedicated attention from your care worker rather than sharing attention with multiple residents.

Maintain Your Routine

Keep your own meal times, bedtime, and daily routine that works best for you.

Keep Your Pets

Continue caring for beloved pets in their familiar environment with your support.

Couples Stay Together

Married couples can remain together rather than being separated by care home admission.

More Cost-Effective

Often more affordable than care home fees, especially for couples or those with less complex needs.
