Personal Care Services

Professional assistance with daily personal hygiene and care tasks, delivered with dignity, respect, and compassion to help you maintain your independence.

What is Personal Care?

Personal care involves assistance with daily activities that help you maintain your hygiene, health, and personal wellbeing. Our trained care workers provide sensitive, respectful support that preserves your dignity while ensuring your safety and comfort.

Whether you need help with a specific task or ongoing support with daily routines, our personal care services are designed to meet your individual needs and preferences. We understand that personal care is private and sensitive, which is why all our staff are specially trained to provide this support with the utmost professionalism and discretion.

Our Personal Care Services

Medication Management

Proper medication management is crucial for your health and wellbeing. Our care workers can help with:

  • Medication reminders and scheduling
  • Assistance with taking medication
  • Monitoring for side effects or changes
  • Coordinating with healthcare professionals
  • Medication record keeping

Bathing and Hygiene Support

Maintaining good personal hygiene is essential for health, comfort, and dignity. We provide sensitive assistance with:

  • Bathing and showering assistance
  • Personal grooming and hair care
  • Dental hygiene support
  • Skin care and moisturizing
  • Nail care and hand/foot care

Dressing Support

Getting dressed can become challenging due to mobility issues, cognitive changes, or physical limitations. Our team helps with:

  • Choosing appropriate clothing
  • Fastening and zippers
  • Managing adaptive clothing
  • Seasonal clothing selection
  • Maintaining personal style preferences

Toileting and Continence Care

We provide sensitive support for continence needs with respect and dignity:

  • Toileting assistance and transfers
  • Incontinence product management
  • Personal hygiene after toileting
  • Continence care planning
  • Discreet and respectful support

Who Benefits from Personal Care?

Personal care services are beneficial for individuals who:

  • Have mobility challenges or physical disabilities
  • Live with dementia or cognitive impairments
  • Are recovering from illness or surgery
  • Have chronic health conditions affecting daily activities
  • Need support to maintain their independence
  • Have family members who need respite from caregiving duties

Our Approach to Personal Care

At Aion Health Care & Support Services, we believe that personal care should be delivered with the highest level of respect, dignity, and professionalism. Our approach includes:

Respect for Privacy

Personal care is one of the most private aspects of life. We ensure complete privacy during all care activities and respect your boundaries and preferences at all times.

Person-Centred Planning

Every personal care plan is tailored to your specific needs, preferences, and routines. We work with you to develop a care approach that feels comfortable and right for you.

Continuous Monitoring

We regularly review and adjust your personal care plan to ensure it continues to meet your changing needs and preferences.
